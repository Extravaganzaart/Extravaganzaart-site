Extravaganza Art is my creative pseudonym. I am an artist specializing in vibrant interior paintings and abstract art, bringing emotion, elegance, and energy into spaces. Any spaces.

I am an artist of my own making. No schools, no canons, no borrowed rules. I have one rule of my own: it must be beautiful. Beauty is the only measure.

I paint with feelings, emotions, sensations — inner and outer. I deliberately combine the incompatible: where others see contradiction, I see a spark. That is where my style is born — my own, unlike anyone else's.

I don't paint for everyone. I paint for those who feel. My art finds its own people.

All my artworks are available both as high-resolution digital files and as exclusive physical pieces created on canvas, glass, wood, vinyl or wallpaper.

Digital versions are delivered by email or via a secure cloud link; physical artworks are shipped to the buyer’s postal address worldwide. Published previews are reduced in size and protected. All images are clickable — single and double clicks.

Every sold artwork comes with my personal signature, the date of purchase, and a Certificate of Authenticity.
